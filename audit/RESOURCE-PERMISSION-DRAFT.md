# Pedido de recursos — minuta não enviada

Esta minuta serve para os titulares/fornecedores identificados na auditoria. Não é autorização de uso e não foi enviada a ninguém. O formulário de clearance EA não deve ser usado fora dos casos a que a própria EA o destina.

## Texto em inglês

Subject: Non-commercial football career tracker — digital lettering and kit asset permission

Hello,

We are preparing an independent, free, non-commercial website for users to record their EA SPORTS FC careers. The site would display personalised 3D shirts in a locker room and career museum. It is not affiliated with EA, clubs, leagues or federations.

We would like to establish whether you can provide, or identify the rights holder for, the name-and-number artwork, kit graphics and/or competition badges for [specific competition/team, season, kit and men's/women's side]. We are looking for a no-cost permission; we cannot commit to paid licensing.

Could you confirm:

1. Which specific assets and seasons you control, and whether other parties' approval is required?
2. Whether you permit public display in a free website, including user-defined names/numbers on 3D shirts?
3. Whether fonts, glyph artwork or texture files may be adapted, delivered to browsers, cached and hosted through GitHub/Vercel or an equivalent CDN? Font embedding and generated textures would be considered separately if needed.
4. Whether the permission includes the represented crests, sponsors and other third-party artwork, or only your own lettering/badge files?
5. What attribution, notice, territory, duration, withdrawal and distribution conditions would apply?

We would credit the relevant rights holders and creators, preserve asset provenance, and respect any limits you specify. We will not treat a lack of response as permission.

Thank you,
[Project owner / contact]
